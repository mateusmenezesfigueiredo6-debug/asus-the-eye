# ADR-007 — LGPD minimization and crypto-shredding

Status: requires DPO/legal approval.

Purpose and lawful-basis references are explicit; identifiers are tenant-scoped HMACs;
content remains encrypted off-chain. Retention/holds precede crypto-shredding, followed
by a non-identifying tombstone. Pseudonymization is not anonymization, and on-chain
commitments require re-identification/linkability analysis and RIPD assessment.
