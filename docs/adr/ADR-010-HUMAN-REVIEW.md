# ADR-010 — Human review for consequential AI and promotion

Status: accepted as gate.

Consequential legal/AI outputs and every environment promotion require an attributable,
pseudonymous human decision with status, policy/version and hashes. Purpose, model,
template, parameter/input/source/output hashes, citations, tools, confidence, guardrail,
evaluation and known limitations are evidence; chain-of-thought is never captured.
Automation cannot self-approve or promote.
