# LGPD and blockchain

Blockchain processing must have a documented purpose and lawful basis independently of
off-chain product processing. A hash can remain personal data when linkable or testable
against known input; pseudonyms remain personal data. Therefore only tenant-neutral
batch identifiers, roots, manifest hashes, numeric ranges/counts and schema versions go
on-chain. Tenant ID, actor/resource pseudonyms, timestamps, storage references and
event hashes remain off-chain.

Minimize at collection; segregate tenants/keys; encrypt objects and backups; enforce
RBAC/ABAC and purpose-bound service roles; meter administrative access; retain by policy
and legal hold; crypto-shred envelope keys after approval; append a tombstone without
identity. Rights requests operate on the authoritative off-chain record and documented
linkage. Immutability does not override LGPD obligations.

Before staging, DPO/legal must validate controller/operator roles, data inventory,
international transfer and subprocessor terms, legal basis/notice, retention schedule,
ANPD incident process and whether a RIPD is required. Before any public-chain pilot,
assess dictionary/linkability/correlation risk, chain governance, permanence, costs and
data-subject impact. This document is architecture guidance, not legal advice.
